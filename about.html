About us

A transport company with Polish roots and a Dutch operating base, led by Volkan Seven.

We move freight like it is our own

Seven Transport was built on a simple belief: a shipper deserves one person who knows their cargo, answers the phone and tells the truth about timelines.

We operate out of Warszawa and the Netherlands, which gives us a natural edge on the east-west corridor. We run modern DAF tractor units and treat every load as if it were our own.
